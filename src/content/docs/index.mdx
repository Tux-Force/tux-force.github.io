## Why Tux-Force

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Legal, with real licenses" icon="approve-check">
		No cracks and no license bypass. Only games your Microsoft account owns or your subscription includes will
		start. Account tokens stay on your PC and only go to Microsoft.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A whole ecosystem" icon="puzzle">
		Not a single tool: sign-in and licenses, downloads, the GDK runtime, a Wine build, automatic prefix setup and a
		graphical launcher with a Store catalog. Community compatibility reports come next.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Dual boot friendly" icon="laptop">
		Games installed by the Windows Xbox app run straight from the Windows partition, no second download. xTux
		installs use the same layout.
	</Card>
	<Card title="AI welcome" icon="rocket">
		Tux-Force is built with AI tools in the open, and AI-assisted contributions are welcome. What counts is a
		tested, reviewed result.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Where we are

Tux-Force is early software. **Farming Simulator 25 plays** completely, including saves, ModHub and Xbox Live.
Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 signs in and reaches its services but does not get past its own backend yet. See the
[compatibility list](/compatibility/) for details and the [roadmap](/project/roadmap/) for what comes next.
